About

I am a Master's student at the School of Computer Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Enhong Chen and Prof. Kai Zhang.

I received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science and Technology from USTC in 2025.

My research lies at the intersection of AI for Science, with a focus on generative models, bioinformatics, and computational biology — using AI methods to solve scientific problems.
